## 📊 Project Overview

The **Finance Performance & Outlook Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI solution designed to provide management with a consolidated view of financial performance, profitability, cost control, budget variance, forecasting, and scenario analysis.

The dashboard transforms financial data into actionable business insights and supports management-level decision making across four key areas:

- 📈 **Executive Financial Performance**
- 💰 **P&L & Budget Variance**
- 🎯 **Profitability & Cost Control**
- 🔮 **Forecast & Financial Outlook**

The primary objective is to move beyond traditional financial reporting and provide a single analytical view that helps management understand **what happened, why it happened, and what is expected to happen next**.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

This dashboard was developed to answer key financial and management questions:

- Are actual results above or below budget?
- How is revenue performing over time?
- Which regions are underperforming?
- Which P&L accounts are driving unfavorable variance?
- Which costs have the biggest impact on profitability?
- Are there unusual financial movements or anomalies?
- What is the latest expected FY2026 revenue?
- What is the latest expected FY2026 net profit?
- How does the latest estimate compare with the full-year budget?
- How would changes in revenue assumptions affect profitability?

# 💡 Key Financial Metrics

### Revenue

Total revenue generated during the selected reporting period.

### Gross Margin %

Measures profitability after direct costs and helps evaluate the quality of revenue.

### EBITDA

Measures operating profitability before interest, tax, depreciation, and amortization.

### Net Profit

Measures the final profitability after operating and non-operating expenses.

### Revenue Variance %

Compares actual revenue against the budgeted revenue.

### Operating Leverage (DOL)

Measures how sensitive operating profit is to changes in revenue.

### Break-even Revenue

Represents the revenue level required to cover the company's relevant fixed and variable costs.

### Margin of Safety %

Measures how much revenue can decline before the business reaches its break-even point.

### Variance Z-Score

Measures how unusual a monthly financial variance is relative to the historical distribution.

Values beyond approximately **+2 or -2** are treated as potential financial anomalies requiring further investigation.
